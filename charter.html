Reflection

What we agreed and my part

The charter made me a Developer / Analyst / Tester, responsible for coding, breaking down and estimating tasks, and deployment. In practice I worked full stack: I developed the backend and helped with the frontend where it was needed.

What it taught me

The agreements we kept best were the weekly meetings, replying to each other on WhatsApp and helping one another. There was no conflict in the team: we listened to each other’s views and respected them, everyone got on with their tasks, and it was fun to work together. The charter also named logbooks as our way of recording retrospectives, and that is the part we did not follow through on.

What I would change

Several parts of the signed charter were left unfinished: the decision-making methods and the Agile framework options were never ticked, the escalation path is blank, and the review frequency and last-updated date were not filled in. We never needed an escalation path because we had no conflict, but I would still complete these before the first sprint, so the team has a plan if a disagreement ever does come up.
