PRP370S artefact

Agile Logbook

A record of daily stand-ups, sprint plans and retrospective notes.

Evidence

The closest dated record we have is the Trello activity log (23 July to 29 October 2026). It shows when cards and checklist items were updated, not what was discussed.

October741 board actions

No separate written logbook was kept. The day-by-day cards on Trello served as our daily plan.

Project retrospective

Written after the project, in October 2026. It is a look back, not a record of a sprint meeting.

What went well

  • Weekly meetings, quick WhatsApp replies and helping each other
  • No conflict: we listened to and respected each other’s views
  • We delivered a live, working app

What did not

  • Too long on the business side while other groups were developing
  • We never deliberately chose our tech stack
  • No written logbook, and the Trello board was not kept up to date
  • Our stories described the team’s work, not the user’s

What we would change

  • Choose the stack in the first sprint
  • Plan the technical side alongside the business side
  • Write three lines after every stand-up and a short retro each sprint
  • Write stories from the user’s point of view

Reflection

What happened

Our charter named logbooks as the record of our retrospectives, and the day-by-day Trello cards worked as a daily plan. We did not keep a separate written logbook of stand-ups and retrospectives. I attended the stand-ups, though sometimes my PC had problems, so I often joined from my phone.

What it taught me

Without a written record it is hard to show what we improved between sprints. The Trello log shows when work was ticked off, but not why decisions were made. I cannot point to a decision that went unrecorded, though some may have been noted while I was not available.

What I would change

From the first sprint I would write three lines after each stand-up (done, next, blockers) and a short retrospective at the end of every sprint (what went well, what did not, what to change). The Scrum Master, Tim Davids, would keep it in a safe shared place such as Google Drive.
